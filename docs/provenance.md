# Clean-room provenance

This project started from an empty directory on 12 August 2026.

The implementation, information architecture, source code, copy, prompts, synthetic records, knowledge documents, visual assets, component composition, design tokens, and infrastructure definitions are independently authored.

Public demonstrations may inform only high-level product capabilities commonly used in relationship-management software, including daily briefing, client prioritisation, evidence-backed recommendations, grounded knowledge assistance, and Teams delivery. No third-party source code, translated copy, screenshots, prompts, datasets, generated diagrams, or visual assets are imported.

The final experience requires internal legal and brand review before presentation.
